How the Wallet Works
1️⃣ Sender pays for the shipment via Razorpay — amount is held in escrow
2️⃣ Traveller picks up the parcel and marks it as Picked Up
3️⃣ Traveller arrives at destination, takes a delivery photo and uploads it
4️⃣ Sender sees the photo and confirms delivery — the amount is credited to your wallet
5️⃣ You can withdraw your balance anytime (processed in 24 hours)

How PeerDrop Works

✈️

1. Traveller Posts a Trip

Going from Mumbai to Delhi? Post your trip with available luggage space and set your price per kg.

🔍

2. Sender Finds the Trip

Need to send a parcel to Delhi? Search, compare travellers and send a shipping request in seconds.

🤝

3. Accept & Meet

Traveller accepts the request. Both agree on a common pickup spot to handover the parcel.

💰

4. Pay & Earn

Sender pays securely via Razorpay. Once delivery is confirmed, funds are released to the traveller's wallet.
